## Detailed Brewing Methods

### Pour Over (V60)
Standard Ratio: 1:15
Grind Size: Medium-fine
Water Temp: 92-96C
Total Time: 2:30-3:30

1. Bloom: Pour twice the coffee weight in water, wait 30s.
2. Main Pour: Gentle circular motions, keep bed level.

### Espresso
Ratio: 1:2 (18g in, 36g out)
Time: 25-30 seconds
Temp: 93C
Pressure: 9 Bar

### French Press
Ratio: 1:12 (Strong)
Grind: Coarse
Time: 4 minutes steep

## Bean Encyclopedia

### Ethiopia
Flavor: Bright citrus, floral, blueberry notes.
Processing: Washed or Natural.

### Colombia
Flavor: Balanced, chocolatey, sweet acidity.
Body: Medium.

### Brazil
Flavor: Nutty, low acidity, heavy body, chocolate notes.

## Specialty Recipes
- Dirty Coffee: Hot espresso poured slowly over cold milk.
- Oat Latte: Steamed oat milk with double shot espresso.
- Affogato: Vanilla gelato topped with hot espresso.
