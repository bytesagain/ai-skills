# Word Counter / 楼台字数助手

## Quick Start / 快速开始
Just ask your AI assistant: / 直接告诉 AI 助手：
- "Analyze this article for word count and keywords" (分析这篇文章的字数和关键词)
- "Estimate reading time for my draft" (预估草稿的阅读时长)
- "What are the most frequent words in this file?" (文件中出现频率最高的词是什么？)

## Commands / 常用功能
### scan
Run deep analytics on a text file.
```bash
bash scripts/script.sh scan document.txt
```
